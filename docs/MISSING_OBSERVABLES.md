# Generators of the Missing Observables and Exhaustiveness of the Finite-Fibre Description

*(2026-07-22. Synthesis of the algebraic consequences of the Gröbner fibre parametrization already computed in `jcqft/fibers.py` and `scripts/branch_locus.py`. This note makes the module structure of the observable algebra fully explicit.)*

## 1. The pull-back is a monomorphism of index 3

The map of polynomial rings

$$
F^*:\mathbb{C}[a,b,c]\longrightarrow\mathbb{C}[x,y,z],\qquad
\mathcal{O}\mapsto\mathcal{O}\circ F
$$

is injective (because $F$ is dominant) but not surjective. The image $R:=\operatorname{im}F^*$ is a proper subring. The fraction-field extension

$$
\mathbb{C}(x,y,z)\,/\,\mathbb{C}(a,b,c)
$$

has degree 3 (the generic fibre degree of $F$), with Galois group $S_3$ (verified by monodromy tracking; see `docs/MONODROMY.md`).

## 2. Module generators of the missing observables

Exact elimination yields:

- every finite pre-image has $x$-coordinate satisfying the cubic eliminant
  $$
  p\,x^3+q\,x+r=0
  $$
  (with $p,q,r$ the polynomials already recorded in `jcqft/core.py`);
- $y$ and $z$ are rational functions of $x$ and the sources,
  $$
  y=-B/A,\qquad z=-D/C,
  $$
  with denominators $A=2D_0$, $C=8D_0$;
- the eliminants of $y$ and of $z$ over $\mathbb{C}[a,b,c]$ are **monic**, so both coordinates are integral over $R$.

Consequently $\mathbb{C}[x,y,z]$ is a finite $R$-module of rank 3. Away from the discriminant locus (and after localising at the leading coefficient $p$ if desired) it is free with basis

$$
\{1,\; x,\; x^2\}.
$$

Every polynomial therefore admits a unique normal form

$$
\mathcal{O}=f_0(a,b,c)+f_1(a,b,c)\,x+f_2(a,b,c)\,x^2,
\qquad f_i\in R.
$$

- The summand $R\cdot 1$ is precisely the image of $F^*$ (observables constant on the fibres).
- The complementary summands $R\cdot x+R\cdot x^2$ are the **missing observables**. They take distinct values on the three sheets and cannot be expressed as polynomials in the components of $F$.

Thus a set of module generators for the missing observables is simply

$$
\{x,\;x^2\}.
$$

As an $R$-algebra the full ring is generated by the single element $x$:

$$
\mathbb{C}[x,y,z]=R[x]\big/(p\,x^3+q\,x+r),
$$

with $y$ and $z$ recovered from the rational formulae above.

A concrete polynomial $\mathcal{O}$ lies in the image of $F^*$ if and only if, after reduction to normal form, the coefficients $f_1$ and $f_2$ both vanish (equivalently, $\mathcal{O}$ takes the same value on all three sheets of a generic fibre).

## 3. Exhaustiveness of the finite-preimage description

The combination

1. roots of the cubic eliminant,
2. the rational expressions for $y$ and $z$ (when $D_0\ne 0$),
3. direct solving of the original system on the collision locus $\{D_0=0\}$,

is a **complete list of every finite point in every fibre**. This follows because the lex Gröbner basis generates the full elimination ideal. Sheets that have escaped to infinity (the degree drop of the cubic when $p=0$) are outside the finite-fibre description by definition; targets on the non-surjectivity locus (the cusp $\mathbb{C}^*$-orbit) have empty finite fibre, which is already accounted for by the geometry of the wall.
