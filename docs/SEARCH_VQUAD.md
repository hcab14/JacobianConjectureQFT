## 1. The question

The uniqueness theorem of `SEARCH_11M.md` classifies the **$v$-linear**
class of every weight system $(1,-1,-m)$: for $m \ge 3$ only tame
automorphisms; at $m = 2$ exactly the tame family plus Alpöge–Mathew.
Honest residue: *$v$-degree $\ge 2$ ansätze are untouched*.

Here we attack that residue at the unique weight where a counterexample
exists — $m = 2$ — inside a finishable degree box:

> Does the equivariant class of $(1,-1,-2)$ with $\deg_v(P,Q,R) \le 2$
> and bounded $\deg_w$ contain anything besides tame automorphisms and
> (conjugates of) Alpöge–Mathew?

## 2. Reduction and the $v$-quadratic system

From `reduction_w` at $m = 2$: every candidate is
$F = (P/x^2,\ Q/x,\ x\,R)$ with $P,Q,R \in \mathbb{C}[w,v]$,
polynomiality $j + 2k \ge 2$ in $P$ and $\ge 1$ in $Q$, and

$$
\det DF = \det M = -2\,P\,J_2(Q,R) + Q\,J_2(P,R) + R\,J_2(P,Q),
\qquad R^2\det M = J_2(PR^2,\ QR).
$$

Gauge (target/torus scalings): $p_1(0) = q_0'(0) = r_0(0) = 1$, hence
$\kappa = \det DF = -1$.  (Alpöge–Mathew itself has $\kappa = -2$ and
$r_0(0) = 2$; the target scaling $F_3 \mapsto F_3/2$ puts it in this
gauge without changing tameness / properness / fiber counts.)

With $\deg_v \le 2$,
$$
P = p_0 + p_1 v + p_2 v^2,\quad
Q = q_0 + q_1 v + q_2 v^2,\quad
R = r_0 + r_1 v + r_2 v^2,
$$
$\det M$ has $v$-degree $\le 5$.  The top coefficient integrates
(asserted Wronskian certificate):

$$
E_5 = 2\bigl(p_2 q_2 r_2' - 3 p_2 r_2 q_2' + 2 q_2 r_2 p_2'\bigr),
\qquad
\Bigl(\frac{p_2^2 r_2}{q_2^3}\Bigr)'
= \frac{E_5}{2\,p_2\,q_2^4},
$$

so $p_2^2 r_2 = c\,q_2^3$ whenever $p_2 q_2 r_2 \ne 0$ — the same
exponent vector $(2,-3,1)$ that governs the $v$-linear $E_2$ at $m = 2$,
now on the quadratic coefficients.

## 3. The Jung–van der Kulk hammer ($R \equiv 1$)

Asserted: $\det M(P,Q,1) = J_2(P,Q)$.  Thus on the slice $R \equiv 1$
the Keller condition is exactly the 2D Jacobian condition for the map
$(w,v) \mapsto (P,Q)$.  By the Jacobian conjecture in dimension 2
(proved) every such polynomial map is an automorphism, and by
Jung–van der Kulk $\operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb{A}^2)$ is generated by
affine maps and elementary shears — **all tame**.  Assembling
$F = (P/x^2,\ Q/x,\ x)$ therefore yields a tame automorphism of
$\mathbb{A}^3$.

Explicit positive controls in the script (inverses by composition):

| family | $v$-degree | form |
|---|---|---|
| elementary + linear target shear | 1 | $P = aw^2+v$, $Q = w+b_0P$, $R=1$ |
| complete-the-square | 2 | $P = v+\alpha(w+\beta v)^2$, $Q = w+\beta v$, $R=1$ |
| quadratic target shear | 2 | $P = aw^2+v$, $Q = w+b_0P+cP^2$, $R=1$ |
| cubic target shear (probe) | 3 | $P = aw^2+v$, $Q = w+cP^3$, $R=1$ |

All have $\det M = -1$ and survive `infinity_prefilter` through its known
false-positive class (nonlinear automorphisms).

## 4. The search box

**Default box** (finishable in minutes; contains scaled AM):

$$
\deg_v \le 2,\qquad
\deg_w\text{ of the $v$-linear part of }(P,Q,R) \le (4,3,1),\qquad
\deg_w\text{ of each $v^2$ coefficient } \le 1.
$$

Scaled AM needs exactly $\deg_w \le (4,3,1)$ at $v$-degrees $0,1$ and has
all $v^2$ coefficients zero — so it sits in the $v$-linear slice of every
$v$-quadratic box that contains it.

**`--full`**: linear bounds $(5,4,2)$ and $v^2$ $\deg_w \le 2$, same
stratification, per-query timeout `--budget` (default 600 s), with a
Singular `degBound` ladder fallback on memory/timeout walls.

## 5. Stratification and verdicts

| stratum | definition | verdict in the default box |
|---|---|---|
| $v$-linear | all $v^2$ coeffs $= 0$ | tame family ∪ AM (`SEARCH_11M`) |
| $r_2 \ne 0$ | some coeff of $r_2(w)$ nonzero | **EMPTY** (GB, exact over $\mathbb{Q}$) |
| $p_2 \ne 0$ or $q_2 \ne 0$ (and $r_2 = 0$) | genuine $P$/`Q` quadratic | forces $R \equiv 1$ (GB) ⇒ **tame** by §3 |
| cheap $\deg_v = 3$ | single cubic monomial, tiny lin box | $P,R$ cubic EMPTY; $Q$ cubic forces $R\equiv 1$ (tame) |

The forcing certificates are Rabinowitsch saturations run **sequentially**
through `jcqft.gb_backend` (msolve preferred, 16 GB address-space cap):

- Keller $+\,(r_{2,j} \ne 0)$ is the unit ideal for each coefficient of
  $r_2$ (also with $p_2,q_2$ free at $\deg_w = 0$ in a smaller linear box);
- Keller $+\,(p_{2,j} \ne 0) +\,(r_{1,k} \ne 0)$ and
  Keller $+\,(p_{2,j} \ne 0) +\,(r_{0,\ell} \ne 0)$ (and the $q_2$
  analogues) are the unit ideal — so on $\{p_2 \ne 0\}$ (resp.
  $\{q_2 \ne 0\}$) one has $r_1 \equiv 0$ and $r_0 \equiv 1$.

Combined with §3: every genuinely $v$-quadratic Keller map in the box is
tame.  Combined with `SEARCH_11M`: the $v$-linear slice contributes only
tame maps and AM.  **No new counterexample.**

## 6. Spot-checks

- **AM must appear.**  Target-scaled AM ($R \mapsto R/2$) has
  $\kappa = -1$, gauge $(p_1(0),q_0'(0),r_0(0)) = (1,1,1)$,
  $\deg_v = 1$, $\deg_w \le (4,3,1)$, and lies in the box; asserted
  end-to-end, and `infinity_prefilter` survives.
- **Tame shears must appear.**  The four families of §3 are asserted
  (Keller + inverse by composition); the $v$-quadratic ones witness that
  nonempty GB queries in the $p_2$/$q_2$ strata are *not* bugs — they are
  the tame locus the Jung–van der Kulk hammer classifies.

## 7. Honest limitations

1. **Box-limited.**  The forcing $R \equiv 1$ and the $r_2$-emptiness are
   exact *in the documented boxes*, not for arbitrary $w$-degree.  Beyond
   them the same structural pattern is expected (an $R \equiv 1$ solution
   is always tame; an $r_2 \ne 0$ solution would need to thread $E_5$ and
   the lower $E_k$), but that is not claimed here.
2. **$v$-degree $\ge 3$.**  Only a single-monomial cubic probe is closed
   (same pattern: $P$/$R$ empty, $Q$ tame).  A full $\deg_v \le 3$ box is
   open.
3. **Other weights.**  This document is $m = 2$ only.  For $m \ge 3$ the
   $v$-linear class is already empty of counterexamples; a $v$-quadratic
   search there is a separate (likely empty) probe.
4. **Gauge.**  Statements are in the $\kappa = -1$ gauge; un-gauged
   solutions are recovered by target/torus scalings.

## 8. QFT reading

The uniqueness theorem's residue — "what if one allows $v$-degree $\ge 2$
at the AM weight?" — does not produce a second anomaly class inside the
box.  Genuinely quadratic equivariant data are forced onto the $R = 1$
slice, where the defect is impossible (the map is a tame automorphism of
$\mathbb{A}^3$, assembled from a tame automorphism of the invariant
plane).  Alpöge–Mathew remains isolated: it needs the $v$-linear D1-even
degeneracy of `SEARCH_11M.md` §6, and the $v$-quadratic directions that
open around it are absorbed into the tame Jung–van der Kulk locus.
