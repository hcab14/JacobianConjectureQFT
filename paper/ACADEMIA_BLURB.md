**Title.** A zero-dimensional calibration of field redefinitions for AQFT
and pAQFT: the Alpöge–Mathew counterexample and uniqueness of its defect

**Short description / abstract box** (paste as-is or lightly edit):

> The Alpöge–Mathew map (July 2026) has constant Jacobian determinant −2 but
> is not injective, disproving the Jacobian Conjecture for n≥3. Read as a
> zero-dimensional QFT — F(φ)=J a classical field equation, the
> Bass–Connell–Wright tree expansion its perturbation theory — it is an
> exactly solvable toy model in which loops are trivial, the tree series
> *converges*, and the theory nevertheless fails globally: two of three
> solution sheets sit at infinity over the perturbative vacuum.
>
> Primary interest: what this calibrates for rigorous QFT, especially
> algebraic QFT (Haag–Kastler) and perturbative algebraic QFT
> (Fredenhagen–Rejzner). Exact, machine-checked results include: (i) the
> pullback F* is a monomorphism but not an automorphism of the polynomial
> observable algebra; (ii) “upgrade formal to convergent” is not the missing
> step from pAQFT to a non-perturbative construction — properness of the
> classical dynamics is; (iii) a uniqueness theorem: in the equivariant
> family (1,−1,−m), the v-linear class contains only tame automorphisms for
> every m≥3, so the defect is rigidly attached to the m=2 numerology.
>
> AI-assisted exploratory research; not peer-reviewed. Code and
> machine-checked proofs:
> https://github.com/hcab14/JacobianConjectureQFT
> (paper PDF: paper/main.pdf).
> Archived release with DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21569062
